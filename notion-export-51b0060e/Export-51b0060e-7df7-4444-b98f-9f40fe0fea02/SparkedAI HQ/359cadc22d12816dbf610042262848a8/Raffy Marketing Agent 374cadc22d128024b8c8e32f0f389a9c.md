## **Core Goal**

A marketing engine for Sparked Innovations.AI that reports on industry discussion so that Kat’s weekly thoughts are directed/relevant and then produces channel-ready content out.

This positions Kat as the category authority on building Medical Affairs into the "third strategic pillar" and serves as a “top of funnel” for the sales pipeline.

Operating model is a web, not a funnel: every channel stands alone but cross-feeds the others. This is not a CRM or sales platform, it does not target or message individuals.

## **Channels the engine must serve**

1. **LinkedIn** — 3 posts/week, 1 long-form article/month, daily engagement. Current center of the web; everything amplifies here until the website/content hub exists.
2. **Newsletter** — weekly, paid, MA strategy focus. *Idea stage only, no content yet.* Co-authored with Daniel Snyder (CCO), whose role stays unnamed publicly (he's still VP BD at Propel).
3. **Live events** — ~5 over summer + a September half-day conference. Standalone workshops, OC/SD, workshop/build format. Primary pilot-generation engine.
4. **Webinars** — one ~1 week after each live event, free-to-paid, pharma-focused, optimized for the virtual format (not a live event on Zoom).
5. **Podcast (Mastering Connections)** — bi-weekly guest interviews; 6 episodes already recorded, needs publishing + cadence. Doubles as a warm pilot-discovery mechanism.
6. Email Nurture Sequence

## Task Pipeline

1. **Intelligence/Research:** weekly Monday brief that feeds the week.
    1. Sources: LinkedIn posts + comments (specific names?), x.com (hashtags); peer-reviewed journals + industry pubs (MAPS, Pharma Medicine); YouTube; Reddit
2. **Intake Kat’s Thoughts:** gather original ideas, industry observation, strategic direction, relevant SparkEd marketing messaging, etc from Kat
3. **Newsletter Draft:** Take research + Kat’s material to draft a newsletter in Kat’s voice. The newsletter is the backbone “ground truth” for all other content generation.
4. **Strategy Planning:** Where and when and what to place channel-specific content derived from the newsletter.
5. **Generation and distribution**: Create the planned artifacts, review (Kat / human approval required), automatically post on schedule.

> Raffy’s namesake the *Rafflesia arnoldi*, a carnivorous plant that draws insects and also the largest individual flower, representing the twin purposes of research and attention.
