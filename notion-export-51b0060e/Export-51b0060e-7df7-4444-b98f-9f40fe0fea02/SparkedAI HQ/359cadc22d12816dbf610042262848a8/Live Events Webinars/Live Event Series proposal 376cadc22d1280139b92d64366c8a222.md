# Live Event Series proposal

**Proposal**

Create a recurring executive and operator-focused event series centered around “Operational AI Readiness for Life Sciences.” The initiative would focus on helping pharma, biotech, and med tech organizations move from fragmented AI experimentation toward practical, safe, and operational implementation of AI within real workflows.

The goal is not to create generic AI networking events or broad theoretical discussions. Instead, the forum would blend strategic discussion, practical implementation, collaborative workshops, and community-building around real operational challenges inside life sciences organizations.

The initiative would consist of:

- Larger strategic workshops focused on operational transformation and AI implementation
- Hands-on implementation labs
- Collaborative “vibe coding” / build nights
- A long-term high-trust operator community around AI transformation in life sciences

**Why This Approach**

AI events today fall into one of two categories:

1. Surface-level AI hype and networking
2. Highly technical coding-focused events disconnected from operational business realities

There is currently a major gap in the market for:

- Practical AI implementation discussions
- Operational workflow redesign
- Governance and organizational readiness
- Real-world life sciences use cases
- Collaborative learning environments
- Peer-to-peer operator communities

Professionals inside pharma, biotech, and med tech are actively trying to figure out:

- What AI tools are actually useful
- How to operationalize AI safely
- What governance is needed
- What workflows can realistically be augmented
- How to implement AI without creating compliance or operational risk
- How to move beyond experimentation into real operational value

**T**he forum is designed to solve those problems in a highly practical and collaborative way.

**Strategic Impact**

The long-term value of this initiative is significantly larger than event revenue alone.

The initiative creates**:**

- Executive credibility and authority
- Sponsor relationships
- Strategic partnerships
- Community trust
- Customer pipeline opportunities
- Ecosystem positioning
- Long-term category leadership around operational AI readiness in life sciences

The goal is to become a respected convening platform and operator community for AI transformation in life sciences.

**Event Structure**

The ecosystem will consist of:

1. Strategic workshops
2. Hands-on implementation labs
3. Recurring collaborative build nights (“vibe coding” sessions)

Each major workshop stands alone, but together they create an ongoing strategic forum around operational AI transformation.
