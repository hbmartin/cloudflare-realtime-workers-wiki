Multi-Product Support & User Journey
• Users may cover multiple products simultaneously
    ◦ Example: Katherine covered 3 therapeutic areas, 7 products as MSL
    ◦ Product focus shifts annually based on company priorities
• Platform approach:
    1. Admin sets percentage mix of products per user
    2. User journey remains constant (focuses on soft skills)
    3. Scenario content varies by selected product
    4. Users can choose specific product for each practice session
• Future enhancement: Custom HCP persona creation
    ◦ Integration with H1/Monocle for real practitioner data
    ◦ API calls to scrape Twitter, PubMed, other sources
Data Retention & Privacy
• Hard cutoff after pilot ends (no read-only access)
• 30-day retention with immediate deletion option
• Anonymized analytics data retained indefinitely for training
    ◦ Requires privacy policy and contract provisions
    ◦ Behavioral dynamics data valuable for future AI development
• HIPAA compliance not required (no medical information)
