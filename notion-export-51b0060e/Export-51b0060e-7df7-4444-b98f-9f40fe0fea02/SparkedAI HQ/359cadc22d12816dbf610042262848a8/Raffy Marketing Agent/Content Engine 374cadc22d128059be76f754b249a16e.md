## Tools and Products

- Content Posting:
    - **Metricool**: Plan/schedule across platforms in one dashboard, manage multiple profiles, repurpose long-form into posts. Great for tracking performance.
    - ContentStudio or **Buffer/Hootsuite**: Drag-and-drop calendars, AI caption ideas, scheduling for LinkedIn + others + newsletters. Connect socials and email.
- Content Tools and Converting
    - Letterdrop: Strong for turning blogs/podcasts/webinars into LinkedIn/social content.
    - [WaveGen.ai](http://WaveGen.ai) or Bloomberry: Designed to turn one newsletter into a week of social media content. (Bloomberry has more personal than corporate focus.)
- Video: Vidyo.ai or Captions.ai turn talks/recordings into short clips with subtitles.
- Podcast: **Goldcast** + publisher ( + Riverside?)
- Newsletter Tools: **Substack** or Beehiiv or ConvertKit. Easy to build authority lists, repurpose into socials, and grow as the go-to resource.
- Analytics & Optimization: **Shield Analytics** for deeper LinkedIn insights.
- Data Enrichment and Personalization: **Clay**
    - Alt. Copy.ai: GTM AI platform includes Workflows, Brand Voice, Infobase, Tables, and agents; it also has a Prospecting Cockpit use case and a Prospect Finder Agent that can surface contacts by company domain and role.
- "All-in-one":
    - Blaze.ai: Generates blogs, email newsletters, and longer content pieces. AI handles strategy, full content batches, scheduling, and optimization.
    - **Jasper**: end-to-end "content pipeline" platform with specific "Brand Voices" and an agentic "Content Pipeline"
    - Metaflow AI: Content repurposing via agentic workflows. Write text then manages the creative and the distribution.
    - **Writer.com**:  orchestrate multi-step content workflows, enforce brand voice, and integrate with existing tools.
    - Typefully: Combined AI writing environment and cross-platform scheduler (covering LinkedIn, X, and Threads). Less feature rich than the above
- Misc.
    - Meet Sona: Built for founders; turns 10-minute guided voice interviews into weeks of multi-channel content (e.g., social posts, emails).
    - Averi: Content engine for startups: strategy, SEO-optimized long-form, publishing. Drives thought leadership authority but more blog/SEO-focused than broad social scheduling.

## "Kat's Branded Voice" doc

Single source of truth every agent must read before producing output, so context isn't re-supplied each time. Must contain: identity/positioning, voice standard (with live text examples), the protected MA Intelligence OS framework (internal-only, not for publication), audience profiles, competitive positioning (MSL Mastery, insights vendors, LMS), content pillars + topic map, funnel/CTA logic, and a dated living-updates section. Agent prompts must hard-stop if output contradicts the identity/voice/competitive sections.
