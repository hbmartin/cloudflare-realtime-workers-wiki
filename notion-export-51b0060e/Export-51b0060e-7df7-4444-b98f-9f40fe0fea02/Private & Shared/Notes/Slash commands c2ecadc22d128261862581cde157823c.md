# Slash commands

Created time: May 7, 2026 11:25 AM

<aside>

Type **`/`** anywhere on a page to open the *slash commands* menu.

- Use it to quickly add blocks like headings, bullet lists, to-dos, and dividers
- Keep typing to search (for example, `/todo`, `/callout`, or `/table`)
- Use the arrow keys and **Enter** to insert your selection
- You can use Notion AI to draft anything, try it with `cmd J`
</aside>
